    More than trash valet

    Six services. One crew. One phone number.

    Most communities juggle a valet trash company, a landscaper, a porter and a pet-waste vendor. We cover all of it under one service agreement, so nothing falls between contracts.

    The Xceptional standard

    What every visit includes

    • Checklist-driven service

      Each crew works from your property's written scope. The same checklist every visit, no improvising.

    • Photo proof

      Dumpster pads, dog stations and problem spots are photographed so you can see the work without walking the site.

    • Violation flagging

      Overfilled bins, loose bags and lease-rule violations are logged with unit and time, ready for your office to act on.

    • A real point of contact

      One account lead who knows your community and answers when a resident complaint comes in.

    How it works

    From first walkthrough to first night of service

    1. 01

      Site walkthrough

      We walk the property with your manager: buildings, breezeways, compactor, pet areas and problem spots.

    2. 02

      Written scope

      You get a scope with the services, frequency and pickup window spelled out, priced for your unit count.

    3. 03

      Resident launch

      We provide resident rules and a launch notice your office can send, so pickup night one goes smoothly.

    4. 04

      Nightly reporting

      Every service night ends with a report: doors serviced, issues found, photos attached.
