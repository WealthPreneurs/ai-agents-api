# Corporate Partnership & Pitch Frameworks

Phase 5 (Impact). Every pitch is built around four points: ESG alignment, keeping
employees housed through Rent-To-Own (RTO), seller finance models that need little
capital, and goodwill in the community.

| Structure | What we offer | What the partner gets | Our revenue line |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Joint Ventures** | Deal sourcing, rehab, and management | Shared equity and a measurable housing impact | Assignment Fees / JV split |
| **Workforce Housing Initiatives** | RTO homes set aside for the partner's employees | Better retention and an ESG "S" metric to report | Rent-To-Own Installments |
| **Seller Buyouts & Revamps** | Buy and rehab distressed or aging inventory | Clears liabilities off the books, revitalizes the area | Seller Finance Income (Monthly Notes) |
| **Property Access Agreements** | Lawn care, handyman, and upkeep on partner land | Maintained property without hiring staff | Lawn Care / Handyman Services |

## Proposal outline

1. Partner problem (retention, idle land, ESG reporting gaps)
2. Recommended structure from the table above
3. Capital required from each side (lead with the capital-light seller finance option)
4. Impact metrics: homes created, employees housed, dollars spent locally
5. Timeline mapped to the 5-phase roadmap
6. Fee: billed as Consulting / Proposal Fees
