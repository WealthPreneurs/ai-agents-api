    How it works

    Plain steps, no surprises

    If you’re selling

    1. 01

      Answer twelve questions

      One at a time, about four minutes. Nothing is public and there’s no listing agreement to sign.

    2. 02

      We read it ourselves

      Usually the same day. If something is unclear we call and ask rather than guess.

    3. 03

      Your details go to matching buyers

      Only buyers whose profile fits your home: area, budget, condition and lot type. We are a referral service, not the buyer, and we tell you who we sent it to.

    4. 04

      You talk terms directly

      Offers, inspections and closing happen between you and the buyer. You owe us nothing either way.

    If you’re buying

    1. 01

      Build a profile

      Budget, counties, lot type, condition tolerance and how you pay.

    2. 02

      Get matched homes only

      No mass blasts. If it doesn’t fit your criteria you don’t hear from us.

    3. 03

      Move fast when it fits

      Full packet with photos, condition notes, lot terms and the seller’s timeline, so you can decide the same day.

    About

    Nelsons Property Group LLC

    We’re a small family business working the manufactured housing market in Texas. Most of the sellers who call us don’t want a sign in the yard or a six month listing. They want somebody to look at what they’ve got, tell them straight what it’s likely worth, and say who might actually buy it.

    We’re not the buyer. We don’t charge sellers a thing and we don’t post your home on a public page. What we do is read every submission ourselves and get it in front of the few people in our network who work your area and buy homes in the shape yours is in.

    If we can’t help, we’ll say so. Some homes just aren’t what anybody in our network is buying right now, and hearing that in a day beats sitting on a list for a month. When we can, we’ll point you toward someone who can help.
